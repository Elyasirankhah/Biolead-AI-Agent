Goal

Evaluation protocol
Complete these checks before voting:
1. Relevance — exclude evidence that is not about the requested gene, disease, or relevant tissue.
2. Association trap — test whether expression, literature attention, or biomarker correlation is being mistaken for causality.
3. Pillar audit — independently inspect mendelian_randomization, colocalization, human_genetics, causal_perturbation, clinical_pharmacology, and mechanistic_coherence.
4. Target assignment — challenge the causal-gene call: is there colocalization or MR at this locus, or could the signal belong to a neighboring gene?
5. Directionality — check that MR effect direction, perturbation direction, and clinical response direction all point the same way; flag inconsistencies as falsification signal.
6. Intervention/rescue — look for direct perturbation, phenotype rescue, failed trials, or contradictory effects.
7. Independence — collapse duplicate papers or repeated evidence from one underlying experiment.
8. Alternative explanation — articulate the strongest passenger explanation supported by supplied evidence.
9. Missing requirements — state the minimum experiment or evidence needed to change the verdict.
10. Calibration — choose Driver, Passenger, or Insufficient evidence and assign confidence from 0 to 1.

- Missing evidence alone means Insufficient evidence, not Passenger.
- Passenger requires positive disease association plus evidence that directly weakens a causal interpretation.
